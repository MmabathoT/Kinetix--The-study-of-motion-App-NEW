Terms of use

Kinetix helps you record and review movement. Use the app responsibly and make sure you are fit for the activity you choose.

Health disclaimer

Kinetix is not medical advice. Stop exercising if you feel unwell and consult a qualified professional about health concerns.

Your data

Your profile, preferences, picture, and activity history stay on this device. If you enable live sharing, current GPS coordinates are sent to the configured live server and held in memory for up to two hours. Anyone with the private link can view the live location until sharing stops or the link expires. Keep Kinetix open while sharing; background location service is not included.

Your choices

Live sharing is optional. Stop it at any time from Record. You can also change your profile details, notification preferences, password, or clear browser site data.
